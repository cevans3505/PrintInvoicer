PrintInvoicer 3.5
=================

Tracks 3D printing jobs and makes PDF invoices, for FDM and resin printers.

Install
-------
1. Copy PrintInvoicer.exe anywhere you like, for example a folder in Documents. Nothing else needs installing.
2. Run it. Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher: choose More info, then Run anyway.
3. Right-click it on the taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar if you want it handy.

Your jobs and settings are saved in Documents\PrintInvoicer. The app copies them to Documents\PrintInvoicer\Backups
once a day (the newest 14 are kept). For safety against a failed drive, set Settings > Backups > Back up to
a OneDrive, Dropbox or network folder. Restore a backup... on the same tab puts an old copy back.

First time
----------
1. Settings tab: enter your name and contact line (they head every invoice), the default customer (who new jobs are
   billed to), payment terms and your electricity price, then Save settings.
2. Materials tab: add your resins with the bottle price and size. A resin whose name appears in the slicer's resin
   profile is picked automatically. Delete the example resin when you are done.
3. Jobs tab, New job: drag in a sliced .goo file (ELEGOO Saturn and Mars printers). The first one sets up your printer
   with starting rates. Check them on the Printers tab: machine $/hr, watts, waste % and markup %.
4. Printers tab, Extra costs: add anything else you charge, such as IPA and gloves per print, film wear per print hour,
   or wash and cure per job. Each one can be switched off for a single job.

For slicer files the app cannot read (CHITUBOX .ctb, Anycubic, Lychee and others), use Add plate by hand in the job
window and type in the print time and mL of resin.

Resin files do not record how many parts are on the plate. Each resin plate asks you to set the count (or confirm
it is right), and finalizing warns until you do, since the per-piece price depends on it.

Finalizing an invoice dates it and locks in its prices, so it never changes after you send it. Revert to draft to
change it; it is then priced at your current rates again.

Updates
-------
The app tells you when there is a new version: a bar appears at the top of the window (it checks at most once a
day, and can be turned off in Settings). New versions are posted at https://github.com/cevans3505/PrintInvoicer/releases. Close the app, download the
newest zip, and replace your PrintInvoicer.exe with the one inside. Your jobs and settings are kept, since they live
in Documents\PrintInvoicer, not next to the exe. Upgrades convert your data automatically.

The source code is at https://github.com/cevans3505/PrintInvoicer (MIT license) if you want to fork it and change it.
